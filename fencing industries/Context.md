## Product Overview

**One-liner:**  
Fencing Industries is New Zealand’s trusted specialist fencing and perimeter contractor—delivering engineered high-security defence enclosures, live substation perimeters, railway corridor barriers, commercial automated gates, and sports facility fencing across Canterbury and the South Island with over 30 years of hands-on expertise and SiteWise Gold certification.

**What it does:**  
Fencing Industries (operating online at [fencingindustries.co.nz](https://www.fencingindustries.co.nz/)) manufactures, fabricates, supplies, and installs high-performance perimeter security, commercial enclosures, and access systems for Tier-1 civil construction contractors, government defence agencies, regional utility providers, municipal councils, educational institutions, and commercial asset managers. Operating from their Canterbury headquarters at 147b Ellesmere Road, Prebbleton 7674, Fencing Industries manages complex, high-risk fencing installations—ranging from 7-metre-high internal military camp enclosures and 13-metre automated cantilever gates to 2,000+ metre railway corridor mesh, electric wildlife-mitigation substation fences, tennis courts, impact bollards, and architectural panel solutions.

**Product category:**  
New Zealand commercial fencing contractor, Canterbury high-security fencing specialist, industrial perimeter security, substation and infrastructure fencing NZ, military and defence fencing installer, automated commercial cantilever gates, sports facility and tennis court fencing Canterbury, SiteWise Gold fencing contractor.

**Product type:**  
High-end commercial trade subcontractor, industrial perimeter fabricator, and turn-key security installer (providing end-to-end design, custom steel/aluminium gate fabrication, compliance engineering, on-site installation, and safety-certified project management).

**Business model:**  
Specialist trade contractor generating revenue through commercial project tenders, subcontractor packages with Tier-1 builders, scheduled infrastructure supply-and-install contracts, linear-metre (`lm`) boundary installations, and custom fabrication fees for automated gate and barrier systems:
- **Commercial & Infrastructure Subcontracts:** Fixed-price tender packages and milestone-based subcontractor agreements on major civil, defence, energy, and transport infrastructure projects (e.g., Leighs Construction, Downer, Hawkins, Martin Civil Construction, Protranz).
- **Institutional & Council Procurement:** Tender-based and scheduled contracts for schools, tertiary campuses, regional sports facilities, and municipal parks (e.g., Kaikōura District Council, Ministry of Education-compliant boundaries).
- **Industrial & Commercial Direct Contracts:** Per-metre and custom quote installation of internal warehouse security cages, boundary chain link, security panel systems, crash-rated bollards, and safety rails for private business owners and commercial property developers.
- **Custom Gate Fabrication & Automation:** Engineering, workshop fabrication, powder coating, motorization, and access-control commissioning of bespoke swing, sliding, and cantilever gate systems.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:**  
Perimeter security and commercial fencing cannot afford to fail—whether preventing wildlife-induced power outages on a critical electrical substation, maintaining strict border control on a military base, or safeguarding hundreds of school children. Yet most general fencing contractors lack the engineering capacity, SiteWise Gold safety certification, custom fabrication workshops, and heavy machinery required to deliver large-scale, high-risk infrastructure projects to specification.

**Why alternatives fall short:**  
- **Small Residential Fencers / "Man-with-a-Ute" Contractors:** Incapable of working on commercial infrastructure sites due to lack of health and safety documentation, lack of liability insurance, no SiteWise certification, and zero capacity to manufacture 13m automated gates or drill hundreds of commercial post holes through alluvial gravels.
- **National Brokered Fencing Resellers:** Act as middlemen who outsource fabrication and installation to untested third-party subcontractors, leading to miscommunication, schedule blowouts, poor site supervision, and fragmented accountability.
- **Generic Steel Fabricators:** Can weld a gate in a workshop but have no expertise in civil ground conditions, trenching, automated gate motor electronics, electric fence energizers, or step-and-touch substation earthing.
- **Cheap Imported Pre-Fabricated Fence Panels:** Use thin-gauge steel and poor electroplating that rusts, bows, and degrades within 3 to 5 years under Canterbury’s high UV and coastal winds.

**What it costs them:**  
- **Tens of thousands of dollars per hour** in project delay penalties (liquidated damages) if boundary or rail corridor fencing falls behind schedule on a Tier-1 project.
- **Catastrophic power outages and wildlife damage** ($100k+ equipment damage) if an uncertified fence allows possums or unauthorized intruders into a live 66kV/110kV substation.
- **Subcontractor ejection from site** by WorkSafe or Tier-1 safety officers due to safety breaches, missing JSAs/SWMS, or lack of SiteWise prequalification.
- **Constant operational headaches:** Gate motors burning out, sliding gates derailing in snow or gravel, and chain link sagging into weed-choked hazards.

**Emotional tension:**  
Tier-1 project managers carry intense stress over safety liability, council signoffs, and critical-path delays. A fencing subcontractor who doesn't turn up, operates unsafely, or fails QA creates acute anxiety. Similarly, school principals worry constantly about child security, while facility managers fear warehouse break-ins and perimeter liability.

- **National Commercial Perimeter Providers (e.g., Hampden Fence, Boundaryline, Warner Fencing):** Large corporate and national operations. *Fall short because:* Higher administrative overheads, less agility for fast-turnaround custom fabrications, and impersonal client service compared to Fencing Industries' hands-on, owner-operated Canterbury team.

**Secondary:**  
- **General Civil & Construction Subcontractors:** Civil contractors who attempt to install their own simple boundary fences using site laborers. *Fall short because:* Fencing requires specialized equipment (post drivers, core drills, tensioners, weld testing) and deep knowledge of gate automation geometry; civil crews take three times longer and produce inferior tension and alignment.
- **Automatic Gate & Access Control Integrators:** Electrical security contractors who install gate motors. *Fall short because:* They do not build or erect heavy structural fences or gates, requiring the client to manage two separate subcontractors who inevitably blame each other when a gate sags or binds.

**Indirect:**  
- **Temporary Fencing Hire (e.g., TFH, Ready Fence):** Used as an indefinite stopgap on commercial yards. *Falls short because:* Visually unprofessional, easily breached by thieves, structurally unstable in Canterbury north-west gale winds, and incurs recurring rental fees.
- **Hedging & Natural Plant Boundaries:** Used on lifestyle blocks or rural perimeters. *Falls short because:* Takes 5–10 years to mature, requires continuous trimming and weed control, and offers zero physical security against trespassers or livestock breakthrough.
