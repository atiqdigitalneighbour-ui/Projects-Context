## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:**  
Untreated or poorly treated concrete is porous, brittle, prone to cracking, and continually generates abrasive dusting. Most DIY coatings and generic painters peel off under vehicle tyres within 12 months, while improper grinding creates unlivable clouds of toxic silica dust and leaves wavy, uneven floor substrates.

**Why alternatives fall short:**  
- **DIY Hardware Store Epoxy Paint Kits:** Water-based DIY paints fail because homeowners lack diamond grinders to profile the slab; without mechanical preparation, the paint inevitably peels and flakes off under hot car tyres ("hot-tyre pickup") within weeks.
- **General Painters Offering "Floor Painting":** Apply coatings like wall paint without understanding concrete moisture vapor transmission, concrete surface profile (CSP), or using commercial-grade 100% solids epoxy resins.
- **Carpet, Laminate, and Floor Tiles:** Carpet traps allergens and moisture in damp Canterbury winters; vinyl seams lift and harbor mold; ceramic tiles chip when heavy objects drop and feature dirty, porous grout lines that require ongoing scrubbing.
- **Sub-par Concrete Contractors:** Use uncertified, under-powered machinery without proper HEPA dust control, filling client homes and active businesses with silica dust, and skipping critical grouting and densifying stages.

**What it costs them:**  
- Thousands of dollars to strip and grind away failed DIY paint or peeling epoxy after only a year of use.
- Forklift wheel and axle replacement costs ($2,000–$5,000+ per year) caused by broken, spalling expansion joints in warehouses.
- Failed commercial health inspections or slip-and-fall liability claims in commercial kitchens and retail spaces.
- Permanent silica dust contamination throughout residential HVAC systems, curtains, and furnishings from uncontained dry grinding.
- Significant commercial revenue loss if a retail store or gym has to shut down completely for slow, amateur flooring contractors.

**Emotional tension:**  
Homeowners feel immense frustration when their brand-new garage floor turns into a peeling, patchy mess, and anxiety about whether their living room concrete will look like a patchy driveway or a luxury architectural surface. Commercial managers feel constant stress over operational downtime, worker safety around trip hazards, and strict health inspector audits.

## Objections & Anti-Personas

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| *"Isn't polished concrete cold and loud in a home?"* | Concrete has high thermal mass, making it the most energy-efficient flooring option when paired with north-facing passive solar windows or in-slab hydronic heating—absorbing heat during the day and radiating warmth all night. For acoustics, area rugs and furnishings absorb sound while keeping the sleek, seamless aesthetic. |
| *"Is polished concrete slippery for kids, elderly family, or pets?"* | Surprisingly, dry mechanically polished concrete has a similar slip coefficient to hardwood or ceramic tile. In areas prone to moisture or commercial requirements, we integrate anti-slip penetrants or slip-resistant grit additives to meet New Zealand Building Code D1/AS1 slip compliance. |
| *"Why shouldn't I just buy a $150 epoxy kit from Bunnings or Mitre 10?"* | DIY kits rely on chemical acid etching, which cannot properly open the hard, machine-troweled surface of Canterbury concrete. Within a few months, the heat and weight of car tyres pulls the coating straight off the floor. We diamond-grind the slab to open the pores and apply commercial-grade 100% solids epoxy that chemically and mechanically bonds for 15–20+ years. |
| *"How messy is the grinding process? Will my house be covered in concrete dust?"* | Zero mess. We connect every diamond grinder to industrial-grade cyclonic HEPA dust extractors. The dust is vacuumed at the diamond head before it can become airborne, keeping your home, paintwork, and furniture clean and safe. |
| *"Can old, cracked, earthquake-damaged Canterbury slabs still be polished?"* | Yes! Concrete repair is our specialty. We diamond V-route surface cracks, inject structural low-viscosity epoxy, and grout surface pinholes. Character cracks can either be disguised or featured as an authentic industrial design element. |
| *"Why does the price range between $60 and $150 per m²?"* | The price depends on the level of aggregate exposure (cream vs full stone exposure) and sheen (matte/satin vs high gloss), which dictates whether the floor requires 5 passes or 15–20 diamond refinement passes. We provide clear, itemized quotes so you pay only for the finish you want. |

**Anti-persona:**  
- **Temporary Cheap-Fix Seekers:** Property flippers looking for a $15/m² quick roll-on paint job without surface grinding to disguise a failing slab for a few months before sale.
- **Outdoor Exposed Concrete Driveway Coating Customers:** Clients wanting epoxy applied outdoors in direct sunlight (epoxy is strictly for indoor use as UV rays yellow and degrade standard epoxy resins; outdoor areas require specialized penetrants or honed concrete).
- **Sub-grade Structural Foundation Failure Clients:** Clients whose concrete foundations are actively subsiding or sinking due to geotechnical failure without first having mudjacking or underpinning completed.

**Glossary:**  
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mechanically Polished Concrete (MPC)** | The ultimate concrete polishing method where the slab is progressively ground, chemically hardened with densifiers, and polished with fine diamond resin pads (15–20 passes) to create a natural, permanent mirror shine without relying on topical coatings. |
| **Grind and Seal** | A cost-effective flooring system where the concrete is ground to the desired stone exposure and sealed with 2–3 topical coats of clear polyurethane or acrylic. |
| **Concrete Surface Profile (CSP)** | The standardized measure of concrete surface roughness required for mechanical adhesion of epoxy and polyurethane coatings (typically CSP 2–3 achieved via diamond grinding). |
| **Densifier (Silicate Hardener)** | A chemical treatment applied during polishing that penetrates into the concrete pores, reacting with calcium hydroxide to form hard crystalline calcium silicate hydrate, strengthening the concrete from within. |
| **Decorative Flake System** | An epoxy flooring system where coloured vinyl acrylic chips are broadcast into a wet pigmented base coat and sealed with an aliphatic clear topcoat, providing a textured granite appearance and hiding dirt. |
| **Hot-Tyre Pickup** | The common failure mode of cheap floor paints where the heat and plasticizers in hot car tyres soften the coating, causing it to bond to the tyre and tear away from the concrete slab. |
| **Diamond V-Routing** | The process of using a V-shaped diamond blade to open, square off, and clean concrete cracks so repair epoxies and polyureas can bond securely to solid concrete walls. |
| **Low-Viscosity Epoxy Injection** | The structural repair process of pressure-injecting thin, high-strength liquid epoxy into deep foundation fissures to weld fractured concrete back together. |
| **Integral Coved Skirting** | The seamless continuation of an epoxy/polyurethane resin floor 100mm–150mm up the wall, creating a watertight, curve-edged perimeter mandatory for commercial food preparation and medical hygiene. |
| **Aggregate Exposure** | The amount of underlying river stone/gravel revealed during grinding: Natural (cream only, zero stone), Salt & Pepper (sand/fine aggregate), Medium (partial stone), or Full Exposure (heavy dense stone matrix). |
